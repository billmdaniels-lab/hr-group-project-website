Games

Ticket to Ride

Long Game

Collect coloured train cards to claim railway routes across a map and connect distant cities. Longer routes and destination tickets earn big points.

Scrabble

Long Game Trivia & Knowledge

Draw letter tiles and build interlocking words on a grid for points. Premium board squares, vocabulary, and tile strategy keep matches tight.

Catan

Long Game Strategy

Gather and trade resources like wood, brick, and wheat to build roads and settlements. Smart negotiation and board placement help reach ten points first.

Battleship

Two Players Strategy

Position secret naval ships on a grid and call coordinates to sink the enemy fleet. Smart guessing and patience decide who rules the sea.

Coup

Fast Game Card Game

Bluff about secret character powers to eliminate rivals in a fast card game. Getting caught in a lie costs a life.

Codenames

Best For Groups

Spymasters give one-word clues to help teammates identify secret agents on a word grid.

Happy Families

Fast Game Card Game Kid Friendly

Ask opponents for cards to collect complete four-card family sets. Simple rules make it easy for mixed-age groups.

Uno

Best For Groups Card Game Kid Friendly

Race to empty your hand by matching colours or numbers and playing action cards. Skips, reverses, and wilds keep every turn unpredictable.

Game Guide

  • Fast Games 30 min or less
  • Two Players Head-to-head play
  • Trivia & Knowledge Words & quick recall
  • Long Games 60 min or more
  • Best For Groups 3 players or more
  • Strategy Games Tactics & planning
  • Card Games Deck & hand play
  • Kid Friendly Great for mixed ages
